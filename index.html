when did social media become my parents

When you were a kid, did you ever say you liked something, to your parents, just once? And they decided to turn it into your whole identity. You’d get themed presents for the next five years. And every time they saw it, in a shop, or on TV, they’d point it out to you. It becomes the very essence of your personality to them. Oh yes, he really loves Shreddies. He just can’t get enough of them. He’s always talking about Shreddies this and Shreddies that. We reckon he’s probably going to work for Kellog’s, when he grows up!

Well, mine was cats. I told my parents that I wanted a ginger cat, hoping that they’d get me one. We always had pets in the house. So, naturally, my parents, and everyone they’d told, got me books on cats. But not; ‘How to Raise a Cat’ or; ‘Cat Care for Beginners’. No. These books were just filled with pictures of cats or encyclopaedias about all the different breeds of cats. I got socks with cats on, ornaments of cats, cats this, cats that, but no actual cat. I could see it, a secondary school boy on his first day and for the next five years, I’d be known as the cat kid. It was awful, a CATastrophe, if you will.

So, sometimes you just need to REFRESH
and Start over.

But it’s too late. The seeds are sown. I swear the house cats even started looking at me weird. As if they were saying ‘such a weird kid, why is he so obsessed with us. I feel so uncomfortable around him anymore. Pervert’.

So, you reinvent yourself and pick a new hobby.

But it just happens all over again. Probably minus the accusation of perversion by domesticated cats. So, you keep your hobbies to yourself now. You scroll through socials and one video appears on your feed. One totally unrelated video and within minutes it’s cats all over again.

When did social media become my parents?
